# Kept-branch rot: how long the 10 `full` branches sat, what conflicted them, what would have landed them sooner

Verdict: 6 of 7 hand-land conflicts were pure sitting-time rot — every causing head commit merged after the victim parked (windows 0.9–15 h). The 10 parks were false reds (9× cleanliness scope-matcher, 1× base-conflict), so no in-run mechanism could land them: kept-land never fired (the iteration boundary never arrived — restart mid-dispatch) and 0/10 meet its approve-on-tip rule; the wave-end steward ran once and skipped ("no conflicts"); stall routed promptly but its 6 replacement `*-land` tasks sat in an 8-deep dep chain whose head dep was never satisfiable (below) and never dispatched. Of the three hypotheses only a combined change works; the report names one: wave-end kept-land for stall-adjudicated false-red parks.

## 2. Why they sat: the park causes and the three mechanisms that never fired

Park causes (last `park` record per slug): parks `upscope` (cleanliness unfixable in worktree); steer/validate `gates/cleanliness`; gate-speed/pack-hashes/resume/qa/worktree `empty-diff` after red cleanliness (gate-speed also base lint, one of the 21 pre-existing base-lint errors fixed during hand-landing); prompt-land `blocked` (cleanliness vs merge-parent paths); real-names `base-conflict`. I.e. 9 false reds from the cleanliness scope path-base mismatch (fixed in-run by `706b0099` at 12:52Z, but merge-checks ran on pin `a1bcd80f`, which predates it — see 00643's compaction-config route) plus 1 real conflict.

- Kept-land (`runKeptLand`, `src/kernel/integrate/kept-land.ts`) runs only at the iteration boundary (`boundary-stage.ts:82`, before pack render). i1's boundary never arrived — the restart killed the run mid-dispatch at ~21:49Z (last record 00800). Zero `kept-land` records exist in `full`. Its own R10 comment claims an "end-of-wave merge attempt" (kept-land.ts:109); the call site disagrees.
- Even had the boundary arrived, 0/10 qualify today: 8 never reached review (parked on checks), and steer/validate's approves (00512 @ `37671212`, 00571 @ `b5282ea9`) predate their resolve-tip moves (`12070ad0`, `187a54ba`), failing the approve-covers-tip rule. Widening qualify alone still fails: `mergeTask` re-gates the tip and cleanliness re-reds on the same false bug/stale pin.
- Steward ran once (00417, 11:59:31Z — the same second worktree-cleanup parked) and recorded `skipped: no conflicts`. Correct under its rule (conflict-parked or red-after-merge only, `steward/candidates.ts:58-70`): at that point the only conflicts in the run were steer/validate's, both already handled by in-run resolve turns, and the goal branch was green. No second steward record exists despite the 18:37Z wave end.
- Stall PM decided promptly: 00419 (12:19Z) routed 6 of ours `replace` (land-task files written, `source: "stall: 1"`) and steer/validate `amend` (both re-ran within hours); 00643 (18:37Z) routed steer/validate `leave`. But the 6 land tasks were links 2–8 of one serial dep chain (`prompt-dry-run-lint-land` [parked, unmerged] ← `cap-falls-back-land` ← `checker-every-consult-land` ← `resume-prompt-relaunch-land` ← `parks-retired-land` ← `pack-hashes-land` ← `qa-catch-sampling-land` ← `gate-speed-land` ← `worktree-cleanup-land`). A dep needs `merged`/`finished` or a merged continuation (`depSatisfied`, `derive/shared.ts:63-70`); the head dep stayed `parked` with no continuation, so the whole chain was undispatched by construction, not by luck — final `status.json` shows all of them `pending` with 0 turns — and the restart ended the run with 16 tasks still open.

## 3. The in-run baseline: 2 of 30 conflicted

30 `*-merge-rN` step records, 2 `*-resolve-*` (00519 steer, 00587 validate) — confirming the brief. Steer's resolve merged head `96c0c267` into the branch (conflict in `launch-goal.ts`: branch's `drainHeldNotes` vs head's `landStoreCommits`); validate's merged `10c19c11` (imports-only conflict in `cli/dispatch/commands.ts`). ## 4. Judging the three hypotheses against INTENT's wave-end PM and steward

INTENT pins: kept complete+green branches land "at the next boundary without a new task" (L164, L437–438); wave-end steward acts "only when a merge conflicted or the goal branch is red" (L100, L217); checks may be advisory and "Reviewer and steward adjudicate" (L165); the steward "decide[s] what to hold" with DoD "branch state ready for the next wave" (L232); stall routing (amend/cancel/replace/split/leave) is the wave-end PM's job (L164, `dispatch-end.ts:84-104`).

- Earlier stall decisions — would not have avoided the rot. Decisions landed 20 min–7.5 h after each park and replacements were written the same turn; the rot came after: a serial dep chain plus dispatch starvation plus the restart. And no task-based path beats the short windows: worktree's first causer landed 53 min after its park, qa's 3.3 h after — a replace→dispatch→full-flow→merge cycle takes hours. No INTENT break, but no effect either. REJECTED as the lever.
- Earlier kept-land (wave end, per its own R10 comment) — alone lands nothing: 0/10 meet approve-on-tip, and re-gating re-reds on the false cleanliness. Timing is necessary (the boundary never came) but insufficient. REJECTED standalone.
- A wider kept-land script rule (e.g. qualify on QA-accept + green) — lands nothing without also skipping the false red in merge-checks, and landing despite red breaks L164 ("complete and green") while moving adjudication into the script that L165 assigns to reviewer/steward. A cleanliness carve-out would also symptom-fix a bug that is already fixed (fence removed, fresh pin at promote). REJECTED.

## 5. One change

**Wave-end kept-land for stall-adjudicated false-red parks: when the wave-end PM routes a park `replace`/`leave` with a recorded false-red cause, a kept-land stage in the same wave-end pass, right after the stall stage, attempts that tip (not only at the iteration boundary), re-gating everything except the PM-attested false check; a conflict or any other red parks it for the steward/normal path.**

Why this one: it is the only single-mechanism change that lands the branches inside the causer windows, verified by trial merge: slotted in the same wave-end pass after the stall stage (`waveEnd`, `dispatch-end.ts:219-224`), the 12:19Z pass attempts 6 tips against head `b47067bc` — parks, pack-hashes, resume, qa and worktree all auto-merge (their causers land 12:52Z or later), gate-speed conflicts on `repo.yaml` and goes to the steward, which is the normal path — and the 18:37Z pass lands steer and validate clean (trial-verified AUTO into `1f27dfde`). 7 clean landings, 1 routine steward resolve, 0 hand-land conflicts; only real-names (19:34Z) and prompt-land (21:40Z), parked after the last wave end, wait for the next one — where real-names' base-conflict makes it a steward candidate through the normal path, no special rule needed. It keeps every role intact: the PM still decides (stall routes), the steward still owns conflicts/reds only (L217 untouched), no new task is written (L164's "without a new task"). The one INTENT tension, flagged rather than hidden: L164/L437 name the *boundary* as the landing point, and the boundary pack renders after kept-land precisely so the PM plans from landed state; landing at wave end moves that forward. That needs an architect ruling on timing, but it does not break the wave-end PM or the steward — it uses both as written.

M4b should also note two residual debts this spike does not promote to the change: (a) merge-checks ran on a stale pin after `706b0099` fixed the matcher — a pin refresh on base advance would have let steer/validate/prompt-land merge in-run normally; (b) stall chained 8 landings into one serial dep chain head-blocked on a parked dep, guaranteeing starvation.

## 6. Caveats

- Trial merges replay git's merge algorithm at recorded commits; they cannot replay the operator's exact `git` version/strategy flags, but the 7/3 split reproduces LOG's contemporaneous "7 conflicted".
- All times UTC; hand-merge commits carry `-0500` author dates (+5 h). Durations measure last-park → hand-land; first-keep durations are noted for the two re-parked branches.
- Refs verified present at analysis time (`rev-parse factory-task/full/<slug>`); per the task brief, durations would otherwise be cited from records/LOG, never zeroed.
