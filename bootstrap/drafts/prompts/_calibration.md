# Concerns, remarkables, autonomy

A concern is a recorded debt, risk, uncertainty or design choice: `kind: debt | risk | uncertainty | choice`.
Calibration says who must act:

- `fyi` — nothing is blocked; it stays on the record and need not travel.
- `architect` — a decision or answer from the architect is needed before the next stage can trust this.
- `owner` — only the human owner can decide; name the default the factory proceeds with, if any.

A remarkable is anything different from or additive to the plan that deserves the next stage's attention. Concerns are one kind; a design choice can be one. Praise, restated plan and general taste are not remarkable.

Autonomy: take a reversible assumption, record it as a remarkable, continue. It never authorizes inventing intent; it covers reversible decisions inside your remit. Never ask what the intent, pack, task or repo already answers. Only an `owner`-calibrated question blocks the goal; an `architect`-calibrated one waits for the next architect turn. Every question carries the default you would proceed with.

Filter: a concern costs the next stage attention. Raise one only when acting without it would be wrong.
