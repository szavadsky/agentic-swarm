## Build notes

- Flow data drives derive/next/execution. `reviewRounds` is per flow (default 2); changes before the last round start a fix round, last-round changes merge and the reviewer concerns become a `debt` record, reject parks; blocked/failed/upscope park; infra is excluded from turns and rounds.
- One item budget (`turns >= itemTurns` → park `budget:`); no flow-level retry. No-progress is one fingerprint per completed step (step, status, verdict, tree or spike findings) compared against the whole slug history → park `no-progress:` (the loop incident).
- Merge queue: scratch worktree detached at the current integration head, setup + gates there, `update-ref` only when green, then a hard reset of the goal checkout. Conflict → one implementer merge attempt (same session); red merged gates → one gate-fix turn then park `gates:`. A merge record is started with its planned commit before the ref move so recovery completes it instead of merging again.
- Dispatch: admission holds absolute/per-harness limits and scope exclusion; drain limits (turns, wall) stop new items while in-flight items finish their whole flow; `checks`/`commit`/`merge`/`park`/`finish`/`setup`/`debt` are records; `derive` needs nothing else.
